# What matters to Alex

Write this in plain English and edit it whenever life changes. It is re-read for every
message, so there is no restart. Corrections you make by moving mail are learned on top.

## Who I am
Software engineer. I run a small side business (Example Co), volunteer as treasurer of a
local club, and have two school-age kids.

## NOW: interrupt my phone (rare, aim for 0-3 a day)
- A real person needs something from me with a deadline today or tomorrow.
- Money moving or at risk: a genuine fraud alert, a failed payment that stops a service,
  a bill past due, anything from my accountant with a deadline.
- A genuine new customer lead for Example Co (a business asking for work, not a vendor
  pitching me).
- Travel in the next 24 h: check-in open, gate or time change, cancellation.
- School or family items that need action today.
- A password reset or new-device login I did NOT obviously trigger.

## TODAY: fine to see in one batch this evening
- Real people writing to me where nothing is urgent; threads I'm part of.
- Receipts, order confirmations and shipping for things I bought.
- Appointments, reminders, meeting notices, volunteer requests.

## LATER: file it, I'll skim weekly
- Marketing, promotions, newsletters, review requests, price alerts.
- Social media notifications and automated digests.
- Login codes and notices that match something I clearly just did.
- Cold sales pitches to my business.
- Phishing and scams. NEVER put these in NOW.

## When unsure
Prefer TODAY over LATER for anything a real human wrote personally to me.
Prefer TODAY over NOW unless waiting until evening would actually cost something.
